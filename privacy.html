Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

This policy explains, in plain language, what information may be involved when you use Astra Music. Astra is designed to keep the listening experience focused and to avoid collecting information that is not needed for the service.

1. Information you provide

If Astra offers account features, information you enter for an account may include basic account details needed to create and maintain that account. Only provide information that is requested by the app for the feature you are using.

2. Information used by the app

Astra may process information needed to provide core features such as playback, search, playlists, lyrics, preferences, and account functionality. The exact information involved depends on the features you use and the services required to provide them.

3. Music and third-party sources

Astra is a third-party music client. It does not claim ownership of third-party audio sources. When you search for or play content supplied by another service, that service may process requests according to its own privacy policy and terms.

4. Device permissions

Astra should request access only when a feature genuinely needs it. Permission prompts are controlled by Android and can be reviewed in your device settings. If a permission is not needed for a feature, you should not be asked to grant it.

5. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information handled by the app and its supporting services. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Children

Astra is not intended to collect personal information from children. If you are under the age required to use a particular account or service in your country, use it with the involvement of a parent or guardian where required.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Astra's features or supporting services change. The date shown at the top of this page indicates when the current version was last updated.

8. Contact

For support or privacy questions, use the official contact method provided with your Astra Music release or visit the Contact page.
